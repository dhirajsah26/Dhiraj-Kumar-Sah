I build software that keeps AI agents accountable.

I'm Dhiraj Kumar Sah, Co-Founder and Chief Software Engineer of Zerava Technologies Inc., an AI safety startup in Toronto. We are building a governed runtime for AI agents: a control layer that sits underneath the model so that organisations which cannot hand their data to a third-party API can still put agents to work, with boundaries they can prove.

Before this I wrote Java for a branchless-banking system used by banks across rural Nepal, built records and school-management software, and studied artificial intelligence and machine learning in Ontario. I grew up in the Terai of eastern Nepal and have lived in Canada since 2022. I am a member of Radical Ventures' 2026 AI Founders Masterclass, and Zerava Technologies Inc. is part of the NVIDIA Inception program.

This site is where I keep an honest record of what I am working on and what I am learning. If you are deploying AI agents inside a regulated organisation, or you are a recent immigrant building a company in Canada, I'd like to hear from you.
