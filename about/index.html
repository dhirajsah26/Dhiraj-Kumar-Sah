About

I am a software engineer and company founder. I was born in Mirchaiya, in the Siraha district of Nepal's eastern Terai, and I live in Toronto, where I co-founded Zerava Technologies Inc. in 2025.

My work is the engineering side of a research-led AI safety company. My co-founder, Andrej Ristić, is a mathematician whose published research on architectural alignment underpins our platform, MatriX. I am the person responsible for turning that research into software an enterprise can run: the runtime, the deployment, the integrations, the parts that have to survive contact with a real IT department. I describe myself as learning from Andrej rather than as a co-author of the research, because that is the truth and because the people we sell to can tell the difference.

How I work

I prefer plain language to polished language, in products and in writing. I keep records as if someone will audit them, because in a company that sells governance to regulated customers, someone will. I use AI tools heavily in my own development work and I am not shy about it; I think the engineer of the next decade is someone who can specify, review and take responsibility for work that was partly generated, and I would rather practise that openly than pretend.

Outside the company I have a long-standing interest in Vedic knowledge systems and Kashmir Shaivism, in sacred geometry, and in the history of the Mithila region my family comes from. I speak Maithili, Nepali and English. I think of myself as a bridge between an older generation that ran its own businesses and a younger one that is being told it cannot.
