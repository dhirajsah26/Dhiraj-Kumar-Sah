Work

A company I co-founded, and the jobs that led to it. Most recent first.

  • 2025 – present

    Co-Founder & Chief Software Engineer

    Zerava Technologies Inc. — Toronto, Ontario

    An AI safety startup, incorporated in 2025 and pre-seed, building MatriX: a governed runtime for AI agents that lets regulated organisations deploy agents on their own infrastructure, with local or open-weight models and a control layer that enforces what each agent may do and records what it did. I own the engineering: architecture of the deployable product, integrations, customer deployments, and the company's technical records. The platform is built on my co-founder Andrej Ristić's research.

    Zerava Technologies Inc. is a member of the NVIDIA Inception program. I was accepted into Radical Ventures' 2026 AI Founders Masterclass.

  • Dec 2025 – present

    AI Model Evaluator (contract)

    Outlier — remote, part-time

    Evaluating and stress-testing large language model outputs for frontier AI labs: reasoning, code, and safety behaviour. Useful, practical exposure to how current models fail, which feeds directly into the product work.

  • Mar – Aug 2024

    AI Developer Intern

    Intellsis — Montréal (remote)

    Applied machine learning development alongside my postgraduate studies.

  • Mar – Aug 2022

    Java Developer

    Citytech — Kathmandu

    Backend development on a live enterprise branchless-banking system used by multiple banks to serve rural Nepal through agents with point-of-sale devices. MVC architecture, API development with the mobile team, testing and validation on a codebase where mistakes moved real money.

  • Mar 2021 – Feb 2022

    Junior IT Officer

    Dreams Academy of Professional Studies — Kathmandu

  • 2019 – 2020

    Digital Nepal — Kathmandu

    Started as an intern and was hired full-time while still completing my degree. Worked across the lifecycle of a school-management system — requirements, design, development, test cases — in Java SE and EE.

Training
