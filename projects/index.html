Projects

What I have built, from the company's product down to the side projects that keep my hands on code.

At Zerava Technologies Inc.

  • 2025 – present

    MatriX

    A governed runtime for AI agents, for organisations that need to run agents on their own infrastructure. The control layer enforces the boundaries of each agent and records every action, so that the model underneath can be local, open-weight, and replaceable. My work is the deployable system; the underlying research is Andrej Ristić's. Details at zeravatech.ca.
